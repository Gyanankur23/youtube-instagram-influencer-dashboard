# YouTube Influencer Targeting Analysis Report
## Startup Platform: Creator-Mentor Learning Network

---

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes YouTube creator data to identify the optimal subscriber range for targeting creators to join our startup platform - a LinkedIn-style network where learners and mentors can connect through creator videos, with a public passport system to showcase contributions and earn recognition.

**Recommendation:** Target creators in the **10K-100K subscriber range** as the primary focus for initial outreach.

## Key Findings

### Optimal Target Range: 10K-100K Subscribers

**Why This Range is Ideal:**

- **Accessibility**: Creators in this range are approachable and open to new platform partnerships
- **Content Quality**: More polished, professional content suitable for educational purposes
- **Engagement Consistency**: Maintains healthy engagement rates (6.7%) across the range
- **Growth Mindset**: Actively growing creators who value additional exposure and credibility
- **Authority Balance**: Sufficient subscriber count to establish credibility while remaining accessible

## Strategic Insights

### 1. Market Opportunity
- The 10K-100K range represents the "sweet spot" for early-stage platform adoption
- These creators have proven audience engagement but are still seeking growth opportunities
- They are more likely to respond to partnership outreach compared to larger creators

### 2. Content Suitability
- Creators in this range typically produce:
  - Niche-focused educational content
  - Consistent posting schedules
  - Higher production quality than smaller creators
  - Specialized expertise valuable for mentorship

### 3. Platform Fit
- **Learner Value**: Accessible creators who can provide personalized guidance
- **Mentor Value**: Established enough to offer credible mentorship
- **Creator Value**: Additional platform exposure, credential building, and monetization potential

### 4. Engagement Quality
- Consistent ~5% engagement rate indicates active, invested audiences
- Higher comment-to-view ratio suggests community interaction
- Suitable for building learning communities around creator content

### 4. Engagement Rate
- # Dax Query Used ->
 Engagement Rate = 
DIVIDE(
    SUM('Sheet1'[Avg_Views_count]),
    SUM('Sheet1'[Subscribers])
)

## Implementation Strategy

### Phase 1: Initial Outreach (10K-100K Range)
- **Target**: Creators with 10,000-100,000 subscribers
- **Approach**: Personalized outreach highlighting platform benefits
- **Value Proposition**: 
  - Public credential passport system
  - Learner-mentor connection opportunities
  - Additional revenue streams
  - Professional networking

### Phase 2: Secondary Target (<10K Range)
- **Target**: Emerging creators with high engagement potential
- **Approach**: Early adopter program with incentives
- **Focus**: Creators showing rapid growth and high engagement rates

### Phase 3: Expansion (100K-1M Range)
- **Target**: Established creators for brand partnerships
- **Approach**: Premium partnership opportunities
- **Focus**: Creators with strong educational content alignment
